# Automation #03 — Review & Reputation Engine
**Status: 🟡 Scaffolded — not yet built** · [← Master Catalog](../MASTER-CATALOG.md)

## Catalog entry
- **Problem solved:** Happy customers rarely leave reviews and bad ones blindside the owner. This auto-requests Google reviews after a job and routes unhappy customers to a private apology flow first.
- **Ideal customer:** Reputation-driven local businesses — dentists, restaurants, salons, auto repair, contractors.
- **Setup fee:** $500–750 · **Monthly fee:** $297
- **ROI:** Going 30 → 150 reviews routinely lifts lead volume 20–40% via map-pack ranking; a 10% lift dwarfs the fee.
- **Required integrations:** n8n, Twilio/Gmail, Google Business Profile review link, OpenAI (review-reply drafting), Google Sheets.
- **Estimated implementation time:** 0.5 day.
